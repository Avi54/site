A Programming Question on Frogs

June 21, 2025 · 2 min

Question#

A frog is sitting at the bottom of a flight of 10 steps and scratching her head over how many ways she can jump up the stairs. She can jump 1, 2, or 3 steps at a time. For example, she can cover the 10 steps by jumping (2,2,2,2,2),(2,2,2,2,2),(2,2,2,2,2), (3,3,2,1,1)(3,3,2,1,1)(3,3,2,1,1) steps or other suitable combinations. Note that (3,3,2,2)(3,3,2,2)(3,3,2,2) is distinct from (2,2,3,3)(2,2,3,3)(2,2,3,3), and she only wants to find the number of possible solutions, not the exact solutions. Work out a function for the frog that solves this problem for any nnn number of steps (not just 10).

Algorithm#

jump(n)
	if n = 1 then 1
	else if n = 2 then 2
	else if n = 3 then 4
	else jump(n-1) + jump(n-2) + jump(n-3)
